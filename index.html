    Questions before you start

    Can I use it for a single shop and not only for distribution?

    Yes. You can use only the modules you need, such as POS and inventory for a retail shop, and switch on distribution features later.

    Can my order takers see profit or purchase cost?

    No. Order Takers only see customers, products and stock they are allowed to sell. Restricted financial data is blocked on the server.

    Does it support Urdu?

    English and Urdu are planned from day one, including right-to-left layout. More languages can be added later.

    Can I import my existing products and customers?

    Yes. Import from CSV or Excel with a guided wizard: upload, map columns, validate, preview and confirm.

    Who owns my data?

    You do. You can export customers, suppliers, products, sales, purchases, payments and reports whenever you want.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. The whole system is responsive, and POS, orders, salesman, order taker and delivery screens are designed for mobile.
